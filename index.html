CLIP STACK

An editing studio that gives you your time back.

We’re a short-form studio for clinics, coaches and founders. Send us the long, messy footage you already have — podcasts, webinars, client calls, B-roll — and we shape it into scroll-stopping clips with hooks, captions and sound design built in.

No back-and-forth, no guesswork: strategy, edit and posting-ready exports are handled end to end. Your team ships consistent content every week and holds on to its most valuable asset — time.

How we work

  1. 01

    Send the footage

    Drop a folder, a Drive link or a backlog of takes. No brief is too rough — raw is all we need.

  2. 02

    Hook & structure

    We agree on angles, hooks and the one job each clip has to do.

  3. 03

    Edit & refine

    First cut in 48–72h, then revisions inside Frame.io or Drive.

  4. 04

    Ship & repurpose

    Posting-ready exports plus cutdowns for every platform you use.
